01 / About & Philosophy

People first.
Learning always.

My work as an educator begins with the belief that every student deserves to feel that they belong—and can succeed.

My background

A teacher, a learner, a builder

I began my teaching career at Diagonal Community School District, where I have spent seven years teaching mathematics and computer science. My work has taken me from 6th grade math through Calculus I and from kindergarten computer science through advanced high school courses. Along the way, I have also taken on district technology and academic leadership roles.

Math and computer science have shaped who I am, and I want students to experience the creativity and possibility in both. Just as importantly, I want to be a steady, encouraging adult in their lives and help them replace “I can’t do this” with the confidence to keep trying.

Philosophy of education

Belonging makes growth possible

Relationships come first. I learn students’ names, stories, and what matters to them because trust is the foundation for meaningful learning. A structured, warm classroom—with clear routines, individual check-ins, and daily collaboration—helps students feel safe enough to ask questions and take on challenges.

Equity means holding high expectations while offering different ways to reach them. I meet students where they are, build on what they know, and use scaffolds, choice, and multiple representations to make rigorous learning accessible. Mistakes are useful information; reflection and feedback help turn them into next steps.

Center connection before curriculum

I intentionally invest in each student from day one, learning their names, stories, and motivations. By actively listening and validating what matters to them, I build a foundation of trust where students feel genuinely seen, respected, and supported.

Build routines that empower independence

I establish consistent, predictable routines from day one so students can focus their energy on creative and complex thinking. By co-creating expectations, modeling classroom workflows daily, and reinforcing procedures with clear visual anchors, I eliminate guesswork and create a calm space where students feel secure enough to take academic risks and thrive.

Equity by design

High expectations combined with flexible support ensure every student can succeed.

Growth through practice

Feedback, reflection, and productive struggle help students see progress in themselves.
