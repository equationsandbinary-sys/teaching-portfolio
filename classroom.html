03 / Classroom & Instruction

A classroom built for belonging

Warm relationships and predictable routines help students feel ready to participate, take risks, and learn together.

Classroom management

Structure creates room to grow

I build a classroom where students are known and expectations are clear. Consistent routines make it easier for everyone to focus on the learning, while check-ins and family communication help students feel supported.

10 minutes

Warm-up

Students begin with a focused prompt that activates prior learning and gets them thinking right away.

10 minutes

Direct instruction

A concise lesson introduces or connects the day’s key ideas and makes space for questions.

Then

Practice & check-ins

Students apply the learning, collaborate, and get feedback while I check in and adjust support.

Communication is part of the routine

I make time for individual check-ins and family communication so students have consistent support and a clear path to ask for help.

Instructional materials & strategies

Tools in service of understanding

Technology is most useful when it deepens understanding—not when it is simply something to click. I choose tools and activities to help students visualize, practice, discuss, and explain their thinking.

Explore & visualize

Desmos

Graphing and interactive explorations help make mathematical relationships visible.

Practice & feedback

DeltaMath

Targeted math practice gives students opportunities to strengthen skills and see where to focus next.

Review together

Quizizz

Interactive quizzes bring energy to review and help surface what students understand.

Interactive review

Practice with a little play

Trashketball

A team-based review game that adds movement and friendly competition to practice.

Treasure Hunt

A sequence of clues and problems invites students to apply ideas as they work toward the next step.

Take a Chance

A choice-based review game that makes students weigh confidence, strategy, and what they know.
